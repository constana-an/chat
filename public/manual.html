Team Chat

使用手册

User Guide

一个自己搭的小聊天室：频道、私信、@提及、静音和免打扰。 这份手册讲清楚怎么用，以及界面上那些数字和颜色分别代表什么。

A small self-hosted chat room: channels, direct messages, @mentions, mutes and quiet hours. This guide covers how to use it — and what all those numbers and colours in the sidebar actually mean.

第一次进来

Getting in the first time

1

装 Tailscale，加入邀请你的那个网络

Install Tailscale and join the network you were invited to

这个聊天室没有挂在公网上，只有加入同一个 Tailscale 网络的人才能访问。 装好之后保持它是「已连接」状态就行，平时不用管。

This chat room is not on the public internet — only people on the same Tailscale network can reach it. Install it, leave it connected, and then forget about it.

下载 TailscaleDownload Tailscale ↗

打开后会自动认出你的系统。也可以直接挑：macOS · Windows · iOS · Android · Linux。装完不用自己建网络，等对方发邀请给你就行。

The page detects your system automatically. Or pick one directly: macOS · Windows · iOS · Android · Linux. You don't need to create a network yourself — just wait for the invitation.

2

打开对方给你的地址

Open the address you were given

通常长得像 http://某台电脑:3000。打不开的话，先确认 Tailscale 连着，再确认对方的电脑开着、没睡眠 —— 聊天室是跑在他那台机器上的。

Usually something like http://some-computer:3000. If it won't open, check that Tailscale is connected, then check that the host's computer is awake — the chat room runs on their machine.

3

取一个用户名，设一个密码

Pick a username and a password

第一次输入某个用户名，就等于注册了它，你当时填的密码就是这个账号的密码。 之后再用同一个名字进来，就要输对密码。用户名 2–32 个字符，只能用字母、数字和 . - _；密码至少 8 位。

The first time you enter a username, you are registering it, and whatever password you type becomes that account's password. After that, the same name needs the same password. Usernames are 2–32 characters — letters, numbers, and . - _; passwords are at least 8 characters.

登录成功时如果提示「Account created」，说明你创建了一个新账号 —— 要是你本来想登录旧账号，多半是名字打错了一个字母。

If it says Account created when you meant to sign back in, you have just made a new account — you probably mistyped a letter in the name.

4

记牢密码

Remember the password

没有邮箱验证，也没有「忘记密码」功能。忘了就只能找搭服务器的那个人手动处理。 建议直接存进密码管理器。

There is no email verification and no "forgot password". If you lose it, the person running the server has to fix it by hand. Put it in a password manager.

登录状态是「按标签页」保存的

Sign-in is remembered per browser tab

关掉标签页就要重新登录，这是故意的 —— 好处是你可以开两个标签页，用两个不同的身份， 自己跟自己对话来试功能。另外服务器一重启，所有人都要重新登录一次。

Close the tab and you sign in again. That is deliberate: it lets you open two tabs as two different people to try things out. Restarting the server also signs everyone out.

日常操作

Everyday use

发消息

Sending a message

在底部输入框打字，Enter 发送，Shift + Enter 换行。 单条最长 4000 字。

Type in the box at the bottom. Enter sends, Shift + Enter starts a new line. 4000 characters per message.

@ 某个人

Mentioning someone

输入 @ 会弹出候选名单，↑ ↓ 选， Enter 或 Tab 确认，Esc 关掉。 被 @ 的人一定会收到通知。

Type @ and a list appears. ↑ ↓ to choose, Enter or Tab to confirm, Esc to dismiss. Whoever you name always gets a notification.

发私信

Direct messages

点左栏「DIRECT MESSAGES」里的名字就开始私聊。名字前的绿点表示对方在线。 私信永远会通知对方。

Click a name under DIRECT MESSAGES to start one. A green dot means they are online. A DM always notifies the other person.

频道

Channels

「CHANNELS」是你已加入的，「BROWSE」是还没加入的 —— 点一下就进去了。 右上角 Leave 退出，标题旁的 + 新建频道。 手机上先点左上角的 ☰ 打开这个列表。

CHANNELS are the ones you have joined; BROWSE are the ones you have not — click to join. Leave is top right, and + next to the heading creates one. On a phone, tap ☰ at the top left to open the list.

看懂左边栏的数字和颜色

Reading the numbers and colours

这是最容易看糊涂的地方。下面左侧是它在界面上真实的样子。

This is the part that confuses people. On the left is exactly how it looks in the app.

# general 2
紫色数字 —— 有 2 条消息 @ 了你。这个最优先，看到紫色先点进去。 Purple number — 2 messages mentioned you. This one takes priority; when you see purple, look there first.
# random 7
红色数字 —— 7 条没读的普通消息，没人点你的名。 Red number — 7 unread messages, nobody named you.
# engineering 🔕 23
灰色数字 + 🔕 —— 这个频道被你静音了。消息照样帮你数着，但不会弹出来打扰你。 Grey number + 🔕 — you muted this channel. It still counts for you, it just will not interrupt you.
alice 1
绿点 表示这个人现在在线；后面的数字是她发给你、你还没看的私信。 A green dot means that person is online; the number is unread direct messages from them.
# general
没有数字、字是灰的 —— 全都读过了，没有新消息。 No number, grey text — everything is read.

消息区里的两个记号

Two marks in the message list

今天的部署都正常Today's deploy is green
发布说明放在共享文档里了Release notes are in the shared doc
NEW
@你@you 能帮忙看一下那个测试吗？ could you take a look at that test?

红色的 NEW 横线标出你上次读到哪里 —— 它下面全是新的。 左边有琥珀色竖条、并且高亮出你名字的那条，是有人在 @ 你。

The red NEW line marks where you left off — everything below it is new. A message with an amber bar down its left side, with your name highlighted, is someone mentioning you.

通知：铃铛里会出现什么

Notifications: what lands in the bell

右上角的铃铛只收两种东西：私信，和别人 @ 你。 频道里的普通聊天不会进铃铛 —— 那些交给左边栏的未读数字就够了， 否则铃铛会被刷爆，反而什么都看不见。

The bell at the top right collects only two things: direct messages, and someone @-ing you. Ordinary channel chatter never goes in — the unread numbers in the sidebar cover that. Otherwise the bell would fill with noise and you would stop looking at it.

点一下就跳过去

Click to jump straight there

点铃铛里的任意一条，直接跳到对应的频道或私信。

Click any entry to go straight to that channel or conversation.

读了就会自动清掉

Reading clears it

进到某个会话把消息看了，属于它的通知会自动标记为已读。也可以按「Mark all read」全部清空。

Open a conversation and read it, and its notifications mark themselves read. Or use Mark all read to clear the lot.

每条通知下面都有小标签，告诉你它为什么会出现：DM（私信）、 MENTION（被 @）、BYPASSED MUTE（这个频道你静音了，但因为 @ 了你所以照样送到）、 QUIET HOURS（在你的免打扰时段里，所以没响，但给你留着了）。

Each notification carries small tags explaining why it is there: DM, MENTION, BYPASSED MUTE (you muted that channel, but it mentioned you, so it came through anyway) and QUIET HOURS (it arrived inside your quiet hours, so it stayed silent — but it was kept for you).

静音和免打扰

Mutes and quiet hours

两个功能长得像，其实管的是不同的事。点右上角齿轮可以统一设置。

They look similar but do different jobs. The gear icon at the top right has both.

静音某个频道

Muting a channel

在频道右上角点 Mute，或到设置里勾选。 静音之后这个频道不再弹提示，但未读数字照样帮你数 —— 静音管的是「别吵我」，不是「别让我看见」。

Click Mute at the top right of a channel, or tick it in settings. A muted channel stops interrupting you but keeps counting unread — muting means "don't interrupt me", not "hide this from me".

免打扰时段

Quiet hours

在设置里开启，填一个时间段（可以跨午夜，比如 22:00 到 08:00）。 这段时间里什么都不会弹出来，但通知不会丢，全都留在铃铛里等你。 还可以单独勾选「让私信穿透」。

Turn it on in settings and give it a window — it may cross midnight, e.g. 22:00 to 08:00. Nothing pops up during it, but nothing is lost either: it all waits in the bell. You can separately let direct messages ring through.

最重要的一条：静音拦不住私信和 @ 你

The one that matters: a mute never blocks a DM or an @mention

你可以放心把吵闹的频道静音。别人真有急事 @ 你，或者直接私信你， 照样会弹出来 —— 静音只挡得住普通的频道闲聊。

Mute noisy channels freely. If someone genuinely needs you and @s you, or messages you directly, it still comes through — a mute only stops ordinary channel chatter.

下国际象棋

Playing chess

对局发生在私信里，一次一盘。进到跟某人的私信，点右上角的 ♟ Chess 就能开始。

Games live inside a direct message, one at a time. Open a conversation with someone and click ♟ Chess at the top right to start.

发起和接受

Challenging and accepting

点 Challenge 发起挑战，黑白是随机抽的，不是先手挑的。 对方会收到通知，可以接受或拒绝。

Click Challenge to start one. Colours are drawn at random, not chosen by the challenger. The other person gets a notification and can accept or decline.

走子

Making a move

点自己的棋子，能走的格子会显示小圆点，能吃的子会套上圆圈，再点目标格即可。 棋盘会自动转到你这一方的视角。

Click one of your pieces: legal squares appear as dots, and pieces you can take get a ring. Click the square to play it. The board is always turned to your side.

升变

Promotion

兵走到底线时会弹出四个选项 —— 后、车、象、马。想欠升变成马也没问题， 不会默认帮你变成后。

When a pawn reaches the far rank you get four choices — queen, rook, bishop, knight. Underpromoting to a knight is offered too; nothing quietly assumes a queen for you.

结束

Ending a game

将死、逼和、认输都会自动收场，双方各收到一条通知。 结束后可以点 New game 再来一盘。

Checkmate, stalemate and resignation all end it automatically, and both players are told. Afterwards New game starts another.

轮到你的时候只会有一条通知

"Your move" only ever leaves one notification

每次对方走完，铃铛里的「轮到你了」会替换掉上一条，而不是堆起来 —— 不然一盘四十回合的棋会把铃铛塞满。左边栏对方名字旁边的 ♟ 也表示该你走了。

Each time your opponent moves, the "your move" entry in the bell replaces the previous one instead of stacking — otherwise a forty-move game would bury everything else. The ♟ beside their name in the sidebar means the same thing.

规则是完整的：王车易位、吃过路兵、升变、将军与将死、逼和，以及三次重复局面、 五十回合和子力不足的和棋判定都实现了。合法性完全由服务器判断，所以不用担心对方作弊。 没有的东西：计时、人机对战、悔棋、和棋提议，以及旁观。

The rules are complete: castling, en passant, promotion, check and checkmate, stalemate, and draws by threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule and insufficient material. Legality is decided entirely on the server, so nobody can cheat by editing their own page. What is not there: clocks, a computer opponent, takebacks, draw offers, and spectators.

石头剪刀布和掷骰子

Rock-paper-scissors, and dice

两个凑热闹用的小东西，比下棋轻得多。

Two much lighter things than a game of chess.

石头剪刀布

Rock-paper-scissors

也在私信里，点右上角 ✊ RPS，三局两胜。不用等对方接受，直接开始。 平局不算分，所以可能打满三局还没分出胜负。

Also inside a direct message — click ✊ RPS at the top right. Best of three, and there is no accept step. Ties score for nobody, so a match can run past three rounds.

掷骰子

Dice

在任何频道或私信里输入 /roll 就掷一个六面骰。 也可以写 /roll d20 或 /roll 3d6，最多 10 颗、最多 1000 面。

Type /roll in any channel or conversation for one six-sided die. Or /roll d20, or /roll 3d6 — up to 10 dice with up to 1000 sides.

先出的人不会吃亏

Throwing first costs you nothing

你出的那一下不会被对方看到，直到两个人都出完才同时揭晓 —— 服务器根本不会把它发给对方。所以谁先点都一样，没有偷看的可能。

Your throw is not shown to the other person until you have both thrown; the server simply never sends it to them. So it makes no difference who clicks first, and there is nothing to peek at.

掷骰子算普通的频道消息 —— 会记进未读数，但不会进铃铛，不然一晚上摇骰子会把通知刷爆。 石头剪刀布则和下棋一样：轮到你出手时会有通知，而且会替换掉上一条。

A roll counts as ordinary channel activity: it adds to the unread number but never reaches the bell, or an evening of dice would bury everything else. Rock-paper-scissors behaves like chess: you are notified when it is your turn, and that notice replaces the previous one.

常见问题

Common questions

我打不开这个网址

The address won't open

按顺序查三件事：Tailscale 是不是「已连接」；网址有没有打错（注意端口 :3000）； 最后问一下服务器主人，他的电脑是不是睡眠了。聊天室跑在他那台机器上，机器一睡，所有人都进不来。

Check three things in order: is Tailscale connected; is the address exactly right (mind the :3000 port); and finally, ask whoever runs the server whether their computer has gone to sleep. The chat room runs on their machine — if it sleeps, nobody can get in.

提示密码错误，试了几次就不让试了

It says my password is wrong, and now it won't let me try

同一个用户名连续输错 5 次会开始锁定，等待时间逐次翻倍，最长 5 分钟。 这是防别人猜密码的，等一会儿再试就好。

Five wrong passwords in a row for one username starts a lockout, and the wait doubles each time, up to five minutes. It is there to stop other people guessing. Wait a little and try again.

我忘记密码了

I forgot my password

没有自助找回。只能找搭服务器的人帮你处理 —— 他需要手动改服务器上的数据文件。

There is no self-service reset. Ask whoever runs the server — they have to edit the data file on the machine by hand.

为什么我又被登出了

Why was I signed out again?

两种可能：你关掉了标签页（登录状态按标签页存），或者服务器重启了（登录状态存在内存里，一重启就清空）。 重新输一次用户名密码即可，聊天记录不会丢。

Two possibilities: you closed the tab (sign-in is stored per tab), or the server restarted (sign-ins live in memory and are cleared on restart). Sign in again — no messages are lost.

手机上怎么切频道

How do I switch channels on a phone?

屏幕窄的时候左边栏默认是收起的，点左上角的 ☰ 就能叫出来。 选好频道或私信之后它会自动收回；也可以点旁边的暗色区域，或者按 Esc 关掉。

On a narrow screen the sidebar starts collapsed — tap ☰ at the top left to slide it out. It closes itself once you pick a channel or a conversation; you can also tap the dimmed area beside it, or press Esc.

下棋能悔棋吗

Can I take a chess move back?

不能。走了就是走了，也没有和棋提议 —— 想结束只能认输，或者把棋下完。

No. A played move stands, and there are no draw offers — to end a game early you resign, otherwise you play it out.

消息会一直留着吗

Are messages kept forever?

每个频道和每段私信只保留最近 500 条，铃铛里只保留最近 100 条通知， 再早的会被自动丢掉。所以别把它当资料库用。

Each channel and each conversation keeps only the last 500 messages, and the bell keeps the last 100 notifications. Anything older is dropped. Do not use it as an archive.

安全吗

Is it secure?

密码是加盐哈希后存的，服务器上看不到你的明文密码。但这个服务没有 HTTPS —— 安全性完全靠 Tailscale 那层加密。所以：只在 Tailscale 里用，别把这个地址转发到公网上。

Passwords are stored salted and hashed, so nobody can read yours off the server. But the service itself has no HTTPS — its security rests entirely on Tailscale's encryption. So: use it inside Tailscale only, and never forward this address to the public internet.

有问题、或者发现哪里不对，直接在 #general 里说就好。 Something wrong, or something missing? Just say so in #general.
