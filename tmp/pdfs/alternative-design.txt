A1 — Alternative design: notifications by subscription, not by push
Same boundary, same three connection points. The one decision changed: the decision moves from write time to read time. A message is appended once; a user's notifications are derived from their
standing rules instead of written into a per-user inbox. Components are labelled by responsibility (not implemented).
